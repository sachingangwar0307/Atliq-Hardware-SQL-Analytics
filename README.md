# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance for **Atliq Hardware**, a global computer hardware company, using **MySQL** and **Power BI**.

The objective is to solve real-world business problems by writing advanced SQL queries and creating insightful dashboards that support business decision-making.

This project demonstrates practical knowledge of SQL for analytics, database design, business reporting, and data visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project answers important business questions such as:

- 📈 Which markets generate the highest sales?
- 👥 Who are the top-performing customers?
- 💰 What are the monthly and yearly gross sales?
- 📊 What is the contribution of each customer to total revenue?
- 📦 Which products perform best within each division?
- 🎯 How accurate are sales forecasts?
- 🏆 Which markets qualify for Gold or Silver status?

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- India is one of the highest revenue-generating markets.
- A small number of customers contribute a significant percentage of total revenue.
- Forecast accuracy varies across customers and markets.
- Some product divisions consistently outperform others.
- Net Sales decrease after applying pre-invoice and post-invoice discounts.
- Business performance can be monitored effectively using SQL and Power BI together.
