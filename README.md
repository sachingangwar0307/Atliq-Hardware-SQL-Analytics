# 📊 Atliq Hardware Sales Analytics | SQL + Excel + Power BI

An end-to-end business analytics project for Atliq Hardware, focused on analyzing sales, profitability, customers, products, markets, and supply chain performance using SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

---

# 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes business data for Atliq Hardware, a global computer hardware company.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow:

**Data → SQL Analysis → Excel Reporting → Power BI Dashboard → PDF Business Reports**

The objective is to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can help understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, market contribution, and forecast accuracy.

The project demonstrates practical skills in data analysis, data cleaning, business reporting, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and data visualization.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The project focuses on answering important business questions such as:

- 📈 Which markets generate the highest Net Sales?
- 👥 Which customers contribute the most to revenue?
- 💰 How are sales and profitability changing over time?
- 📊 What percentage of total Net Sales comes from each customer?
- 📦 Which products perform best within each division?
- 🌎 How do different regions and markets perform?
- 💹 What is the Gross Margin and Net Profit performance?
- 🎯 How accurate are sales forecasts?
- 📉 Which products and customers have high forecast errors?
- 📦 Where are inventory risks such as excess inventory or stock-outs?
- 🏆 Which markets qualify for Gold or Silver status based on sales quantity?
